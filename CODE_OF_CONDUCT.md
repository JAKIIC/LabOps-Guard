# Code of conduct

LabOps Guard contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, focus criticism on the work,
and make technical decisions from reproducible evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary. Report
conduct concerns privately through the project owner's existing collaboration channel; do not post
sensitive personal information publicly. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical,
with attention to safety and due process.
